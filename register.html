Hole Sponsorship Details

Hole sponsorship is $750 per hole (in addition to entry fees) and guarantees tournament entry. All golf membership categories may register. Proprietary and Emeritus members receive priority — the first 18 spots are reserved for Prop/Emeritus registrants in order of submission. If spots remain after Prop/Emeritus registrations are processed, they are awarded to other membership categories in order of registration. Hole sponsor registrations close on Wednesday, June 10 at 11:59 PM PDT. All applicants will be notified on Thursday, June 11 whether they have been awarded a sponsorship. Members not awarded a sponsorship are encouraged to register during the Open Registration window beginning June 12.

Member Information

As a non-Proprietary/Emeritus member, you are welcome to register for a hole sponsorship. However, spots are awarded to Proprietary and Emeritus members first. If all 18 spots are claimed by Prop/Emeritus registrants, your hole sponsorship will not be awarded — but your registration will be held on file for open registration.
