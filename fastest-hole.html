Four players, one ball

One player has to hit the tee shot. After that it's up to the team where the other three stand — someone at the landing zone, someone farther down, and someone up by the green ready to chip or putt depending on how the second shot comes out. Placement is the whole strategy.

The ball must stop

That's the only rule. Run to it, yell at it, swing with whatever club is in your hand — but the ball has to come to rest before it's struck. And if you hit it out of bounds, you're done.

The clock, not the card

Time starts when the tee shot is struck and stops when the ball drops. Strokes don't matter. A seven in ninety seconds beats a four in two minutes. Fastest foursome on Friday takes it.
